## Behavior / interface contract

Input remains an intent producer; simulation remains combat authority.

Delivered input behavior:
- permanent mobile surface remains D-pad + ATTACK + SPECIAL + JUMP;
- `ATTACK + SPECIAL` resolves to one `ultimate=true` intent only while authoritative `superReady` is true;
- Ultimate priority suppresses normal attack, normal special and Push Guard for that activation;
- chord buffering is READY-only; outside READY, normal ATTACK/SPECIAL remain immediate;
- READY chord tolerance is 55 ms (about 3.3 fixed 60 Hz frames), reduced from the rejected 90 ms scaffold;
- quick standalone taps shorter than the chord window are preserved;
- SPECIAL in authoritative defensive context (`blocking || blockstunFrames > 0`) routes to exclusive `pushGuard=true`;
- routed Push Guard suppresses attack/special/ultimate on that frame;
- UI/input does not decide GUARD cost, ultimate legality, capture, damage, range or Push Guard success.
