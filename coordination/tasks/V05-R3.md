## Goal
Make short melee routes reliable, preserve inputs through hitstop, add one grounded low per fighter and establish the final Special grammar.

## Required
Follow plan R3 plus contracts C1/C2/C3:
- bounded six advancing-combat-frame pending command;
- hitstop freezes expiry;
- no lost attack edge through hitstop;
- one command/one consumption and explicit priority;
- real claw1→claw2 and nose1→nose2→nose3 combo continuity;
- one grounded low per fighter using existing guard-height rules;
- SPECIAL/forward+SPECIAL = ranged;
- down or down-forward + SPECIAL = Coletazo/Tramontana;
- remove low tongue from selectable kit;
- measure outcome/actionability, not just moveId transitions;
- Specials gain meaningful whiff/block commitment without blanket cooldowns.

## Prohibited
No throw/grab, long combo engine, special-cancel tree or new resource.

## Handoff
Exact tuning table, measured frame/actionability evidence, tests, exact SHA.
